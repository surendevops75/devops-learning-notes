# HR Interview Questions

## Tell Me About Yourself

### Answer Framework

```text
Current Role

↓

Experience

↓

Skills

↓

Major Projects

↓

Career Goal
```

---

### Sample Answer

> I am a DevOps Engineer with over 4 years of experience in designing, automating, and maintaining cloud infrastructure and CI/CD pipelines. I have worked extensively with AWS, Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform, Jenkins, GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, Argo CD, and Linux. My experience includes infrastructure automation, Kubernetes deployments on Amazon EKS, GitOps implementation, monitoring with Prometheus, Grafana, and ELK Stack, and integrating security tools such as SonarQube, Trivy, and Veracode into CI/CD pipelines. I enjoy solving infrastructure challenges, improving deployment reliability, and building scalable cloud-native platforms.

---

# Why DevOps?

### Sample Answer

DevOps enables faster, more reliable software delivery by combining development, operations, automation, and collaboration. I enjoy working across infrastructure, automation, cloud platforms, and CI/CD to improve both deployment speed and system reliability.

# HR Interview Tips

Do

- Be honest
- Be concise
- Use real examples
- Show enthusiasm
- Focus on impact

Don't

- Criticize previous employers
- Exaggerate experience
- Memorize robotic answers
- Avoid difficult questions
